HOW TO FENCE

Move with WASD or arrows. J attacks, Space lunges, K parries (or cancels attack to feint), and L ripostes after a parry. First to 5 touches wins. P pauses; R restarts.

Try: Advance → Lunge → Attack, or Retreat → Parry → Riposte.

REFEREE

UNGARD → READY → FENCE

The referee controls the beginning and end of each point.

A missed attack does NOT stop the bout.

Only a valid scoring touch causes:

TOUCH → HALT → REFEREE SIGNAL → SCORE → RESET

IMPORTANT

Normal attacks, misses, parries, and ripostes do not automatically trigger a referee hand signal.

The referee only gives a scoring signal when a valid touch has actually been awarded.
